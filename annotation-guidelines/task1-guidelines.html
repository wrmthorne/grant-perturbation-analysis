Background & Purpose

This project aims to evaluate the capability of today's offline, privacy-preserving LLMs in relation to the UKRI research grant proposal review process. It hopes to determine the specific proficiencies and failure modes of LLM review systems in order to identify where they might provide value and at what cost.

The Task

You will be given a UKRI funding opportunity document and a section from a research proposal. You will complete two tasks for each sample:

  1. Provide feedback: Use the positive and negative feedback boxes to acknowledge the strengths of the work and provide constructive criticism. You can optionally provide direct evidence in the text by using the green and red highlight tools.
  2. Assign a score: Give a score for the section (1-6 scale, see below). Your score should reflect what you would expect if the entire proposal were written to the same standard as this section.
Writing style: We do not expect full, structured, and proofed reviews. We are most interested in the arguments you put forward and the evidence you draw upon to reach a decision, rather than how that process is presented. We encourage notes, bullet points, and short/blunt commentary, provided your intention is clear.

Assessment Criteria

Evaluate the proposal section against these UKRI criteria (as applicable to the section provided):

Vision

  • Is of excellent quality and importance within or beyond the field(s) or area(s)
  • Has the potential to advance current understanding, or generate new knowledge, thinking or discovery within or beyond the field or area
  • Is timely given current trends, context, and needs
  • Impacts world-leading research, society, the economy, or the environment

Approach

  • Is effective and appropriate to achieve objectives
  • Is feasible, and comprehensively identifies any risks to delivery and how they will be managed
  • Uses a clear and transparent methodology (if applicable)
  • Summarises previous work and describes how this will be built upon and progressed (if applicable)
  • Will maximise translation of outputs into outcomes and impacts
  • Describes how the research environment will contribute to the success of the work

Capability to Deliver

  • The relevant experience (appropriate to career stage) to deliver the proposed work
  • The right balance of skills and expertise to cover the proposed work
  • The appropriate leadership and management skills to deliver the work and develop others
  • Contributed to developing a positive research environment and wider community

Ethics

  • Is the proposal ethical

Resources

  • All resources are appropriate
  • The project will make optimal use of resources to achieve its outcomes

Review Requirements

Your review must:

  • Include enough information to help UKRI staff and panellists make an informed judgement
  • Base comments only on information included in the application
  • Not review negatively due to equality, diversity and inclusion factors (e.g., part-time work, health-related absences)
  • Avoid speculative, inflammatory or damaging comments
  • Not use journal metrics, conference rankings or personal metrics as substitute measures for assessing contributions
  • Not reveal your identity or contain conflicts of interest

Scoring Scale

6 – Exceptional:
The application is outstanding. It addresses all of the assessment criteria and meets them to an exceptional level.
5 – Excellent:
The application is very high quality. It addresses most of the assessment criteria and meets them to an excellent level. There are very minor weaknesses.
4 – Very good:
The application demonstrates considerable quality. It meets most of the assessment criteria to a high level. There are minor weaknesses.
3 – Good:
The application is of good quality. It meets most of the assessment criteria to an acceptable level, but not across all aspects of the proposed activities. There are weaknesses.
2 – Weak:
The application is not sufficiently competitive. It meets some of the assessment criteria to an adequate level. There are, however, significant weaknesses.
1 – Poor:
The application is flawed or unsuitable quality for funding. It does not meet the assessment criteria to an adequate level.
