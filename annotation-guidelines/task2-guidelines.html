Confidentiality: You must not copy, share, or reproduce any of the data you evaluate.
Your Role: Evaluate individual reviewer comments to determine (1) whether you agree with them based on the proposal text, and (2) how much influence they should have on the final funding decision.

Overview: What You're Evaluating

You will receive:

  • A proposal excerpt (the specific section being commented on)
  • A single reviewer comment about that section
  • Relevant review guidelines and assessment criteria
  • A copy of the associated funding opportunity document

For each comment, you will provide two independent judgments:

  1. Agreement: Do you agree with the reviewer's statement based solely on what's written in the proposal?
  2. Influence: Assuming the statement is accurate, how much would it affect the final funding score?
Critical Point: Agreement and influence are separate. A comment you disagree with can still have high influence (if it were true). Conversely, you may agree with a comment that has minimal influence on funding decisions.

Task 1: Agreement Assessment

Judge whether the reviewer's comment is supported by the proposal text you've been shown.

Examples:

Comment: "No contingency plans are provided for equipment failure."
Proposal excerpt: Contains a Risk Register with mitigation strategies for equipment failure.
Your response: Disagree
Comment: "The timeline for Phase 2 appears optimistic given the complexity."
Proposal excerpt: Shows 6 months allocated for a multi-site experimental campaign.
Your response: Agree

Task 2: Influence Rating

Rate how much this comment would affect your funding recommendation if it were accurate

Rating Definition Examples
None Purely factual or administrative. No bearing on scientific merit or deliverability. "The PI is based at Imperial College London."

"This builds on EPSRC grant EP/X123456/1."
Little Minor issues that are easily correctable or don't affect core assessment criteria. "Figure 3 is difficult to read."

"The bibliography uses inconsistent formatting."
Some Valid observations affecting secondary criteria. Would influence score by ±0.5 points. "The budget justification for travel could be more detailed."

"The pathway to impact section lacks specific partner commitments."
Substantially Significant strengths or weaknesses directly affecting Quality or Importance. Would shift score by ±1–2 points. "The proposed methodology represents a genuine advance over current techniques."

"The research questions lack sufficient novelty given recent publications."
Pivotal Fundamental issues affecting viability or exceptional strengths. Changes fundable/non-fundable status. "The underlying theoretical framework contradicts established principles."

"This directly addresses a critical EPSRC strategic priority with unique national capability."

Decision Framework

  • Pivotal/Substantial: Comments addressing core EPSRC criteria (Quality, Importance, Fit to Scheme)
  • Some: Comments on secondary elements (resources, timelines, impact pathways)
  • Little/None: Observations with no bearing on fundability

Key Reminders

  • Base your agreement only on the provided proposal excerpt and accompanying documentation
  • Rate influence as if the comment is true, even if you disagree with it or it is demonstrably false
  • Consider the comment in isolation; don't try to infer the overall proposal quality
